Questions

Frequently Asked

Does IV therapy hurt?

Most guests feel a quick pinch when the small catheter goes in, then nothing at all. Our nurses are experienced at making it as comfortable as possible.

How long does a session take?

Plan on about 45 minutes from check-in to check-out. The drip itself usually runs 30 to 45 minutes depending on the treatment.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead guarantees your chair, especially on weekends.

Is IV hydration safe for me?

Before every treatment a registered nurse reviews your health history and checks your vitals. If a drip isn't right for you, we'll tell you and suggest alternatives.
